# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27-Oct-2026

Expense Ledger is a Windows desktop app developed by Tenwin James. It works entirely on your computer.

## What the app stores

All data stays on your device:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Your entries (Excel files) | `Documents\ExpenseLedger\<YYYY>\<MM-Mon>\<DD-Mon-YYYY>.xlsx` |
| Login account | `%APPDATA%\ExpenseLedger\account.json` (the password is stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, never as plain text) |
| Sub-heads | `%APPDATA%\ExpenseLedger\subheads.json` |
| Log | `%APPDATA%\ExpenseLedger\app.log` |

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, send, or share any personal or financial information.
- It does not connect to the internet or to any server.
- It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash reporting.
- It does not use third-party accounts or cloud services.

## Your control

You own your data. Uninstalling the app removes the program and your login; your entries and sub-heads are kept. You can delete the folders listed above at any time to remove all stored data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this file in the project repository.
