# How to Share the Standalone Dairy Application

A completely self-contained, offline-ready version of the **LactaFlow Enterprise Dairy OS** has been generated for you to share with anyone who does **not** have Google AI Studio or Node.js.

---

### 📁 File Locations
- **`public/standalone-dairy-app.html`** (available directly via browser download)
- **`standalone-dairy-app.html`** (located directly in your project root)

---

### 🚀 3 Easy Ways to Share

#### 1. Send as an Email Attachment or File Transfer
1. Download or copy `standalone-dairy-app.html`.
2. Attach it to an email, or send via Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, or Google Drive.
3. The recipient simply **double-clicks the `.html` file** on their computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, or iPad/tablet). It will immediately open in Google Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
4. **No software installation, no Node.js, and no Google AI Studio account required!**

#### 2. One-Click In-App Download
In the main running web application header, click the **"Shareable HTML"** green button. Your browser will instantly download `lactaflow-dairy-management.html` to your computer.

#### 3. Free Static Web Hosting
You can also host this file for free on any static host:
- **GitHub Pages**: Upload `standalone-dairy-app.html` (rename to `index.html`).
- **Netlify / Vercel**: Drag and drop the folder containing `standalone-dairy-app.html`.

---

### ✨ Features Included in the Standalone File
- **Transparent Frosted-Glass Dairy Theme**: Ambient flowing milk wave background and glassmorphism cards.
- **Header Telemetry**: Silo temperature monitor, HACCP food safety status, and real-time intake volume calculation.
- **Tab 1 — Raw Milk Intake & Reception**: Form with all fields (ID, name, phone, email, location, category, status, volume in Liters, fat %, SNF %, temperature in °C, silo tank, quality notes), validation, edit mode banner, and complete data table.
- **Tab 2 — Processing & Logistics**: Secondary sub-tabs for Pasteurization & Lab Quality Testing (Sub-tab 1) and Cold-Chain Logistics (Sub-tab 2) with independent records and filters.
- **Full Interactivity**: Live search, category and status filters, date range filters, column sorting, pagination, and deletion modal.
- **Persistence**: Automatically saves all additions and edits to the user's browser `localStorage`, persisting across page refreshes.
- **Exporting**: Click **"Export CSV"** to export records into spreadsheet format anytime.
